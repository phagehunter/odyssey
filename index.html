Odyssey Character Map

A visual companion to Homer's epic, by Curtis Hoffmann

This is a single-page dashboard for reading Homer's Odyssey two ways at once: at a distance, through visualizations built from the poem's 53 named characters and groups, 17 real and mythic places and the interactions between them; and up close, through commentary with line citations and the complete Samuel Butler translation (1900) readable beside every view.

Use the book slider (1 to 24) and the group toggles (Ithacan loyalists, the suitors, the gods, foreign peoples and monsters) to re-draw every view for any stretch of the story. Click a character, an edge, a route or a waypoint to open the commentary panel; each citation there links into the text reader.

What the three views show

  • Network Dynamics. A force-directed graph of who meets, helps or fights whom, including divine interventions. Node size follows how connected a character is; a playback control animates the network book by book. The 108 suitors are drawn as their two leaders plus regional groups.
  • Spatial Topography. A map of the journeys of Odysseus, Telemachus and the divine messengers. Solid lines are explicit routes; dashed lines are inferred or mythic. Waypoints are marked historical, contested or mythological, because the locations of Ogygia, Aeaea and the Underworld are speculative by design.
  • Narrative Gravity. A stream graph of whose point of view each book follows (Telemachus, Odysseus, Penelope, the gods, others), with a dual-timeline overlay for Books 9 to 12, where Odysseus narrates his own flashback at the Phaeacian court.

Focalization shares and interaction weights are editorial estimates made for visualization, not word counts. The application needs JavaScript; it is loading now, or read the source on GitHub.
